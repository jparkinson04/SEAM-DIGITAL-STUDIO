QUESTIONS

Things people actually ask.

If your question isn't here, message us. We answer everything.

How long does the setup take?

−

Two weeks from discovery call to live site, in most cases. We'll give you a firm timeline upfront so you know exactly when you're going live.

Do I have to use a specific booking system?

+

No. We integrate with whatever you already use — Ticket Tailor, Fresha, Treatwell, Square, or anything else. If you don't have one yet, we'll recommend the right fit for your business.

Am I locked into the retainer?

+

No long-term contracts. The monthly retainer is rolling — cancel any time with 30 days' notice. Most clients stay because the value's clear, not because they have to.

What if I already have a website?

+

If your existing site works for you, great — we can connect everything else around it. If it isn't pulling its weight, we'll build something that does. The discovery call covers exactly this.

Who do you work with?

+

Local beauty, wellness, and salon businesses, mostly within 30 minutes of Doncaster. Independent owners, small teams, and anyone who wants their digital setup to actually feel sorted.
